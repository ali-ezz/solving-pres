How to read this

No protection? Then the same ID comes back on every visit — even in incognito, even after clearing cookies.

With protection the page detects canvas noise and spoofed values (GPU, screen, time zone…), and WebRTC can no longer reveal your IP. Values may stay the same on reload here but differ on every other site, so sites cannot link your visits.

"Switched on late" means the protection arrived after the page’s first scripts ran — real trackers run that early too.

Nothing on this page is stored or uploaded. The only network request is the WebRTC test itself.
